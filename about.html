Our History & Legacy

Founded by the Franciscan Missionary Sisters of Our Lady of Perpetual Help, Holy Childhood High School (located at 9 Skibo Avenue, Kingston 10) has stood as a pillar of academic excellence, discipline, and holistic development for young women in Jamaica.

Landmark Accomplishments: Consistently ranked among Jamaica's top secondary institutions for CSEC and CAPE pass rates, national performing arts medals, and youth leadership.

Academics & Curriculum

Offering a comprehensive lower-school foundation (Grades 7–9) leading into CSEC (Grades 10–11) and CAPE Sixth Form programs across Sciences, Business, Humanities, and Information Technology.

Admissions & Operations

Location: 9 Skibo Avenue, Kingston 10
Office Hours: Mon–Fri, 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM
Admissions: Primary entry via PEP placement; Sixth Form applications open annually in June.
